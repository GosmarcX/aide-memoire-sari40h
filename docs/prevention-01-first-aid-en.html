First aid
What changes when help is far away
The first aider's objective: to provide the best possible care, as quickly as possible, without creating new casualties.
Urban setting Wilderness
Access to care Minutes Hours to days
Resources Available, specialized Limited, improvised
Communications Easy, 9-1-1 Limited, local numbers
Environment Controlled Hostile, changing
Figure 1. How delayed access to care changes your role.
1
Four basic principles, everywhere and always. Ensure the safety of everyone present, prevent further injury, perform life-saving interventions, and hand over responsibility for care to prehospital services as soon as possible.
2
Urban setting and backcountry: two realities. See the figure. Delay transforms the role: you are not just stabilizing while you wait, you are taking charge of care.
3
Know the local emergency network. Ground emergency medical services (first responders, paramedics), air EMS, fire, police, search and rescue. 9-1-1 does not work everywhere: the numbers that actually work must be in the emergency plan.
4
First aider or health care professional. Level of training and context dictate the degree of care to provide. The first aider must recognize their own limits when faced with a critically injured patient.
5
The qualities expected of a first aider. Stay calm, organized and diligent, act professionally, keep your skills current, and be a role model through a healthy and safe lifestyle.
