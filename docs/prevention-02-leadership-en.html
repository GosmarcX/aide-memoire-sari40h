Leadership
Your responsibilities in prevention and risk management
The leader is anyone responsible for a group in a remote setting: expedition leader, guide, camp director, worksite supervisor.
Figure 2 — La prévention se joue avant le départ, dans cet ordre.
1
A competent leader, a clear role. Physical fitness, experience and training suited to the activity. When several leaders supervise a group, roles are defined in advance. The leader-to-participant ratio follows the guidelines for the activity.
2
Match the group to the activity. A mismatch between members' abilities and the demands of the trip is a frequent cause of accidents. Pre-trip meetings and medical forms allow problems to be anticipated, rarely to exclude anyone.
3
A risk management plan. Assess the severity of risks, then prevent, control, reduce or eliminate them. Inform clients and workers of inherent risks, ideally through a safety briefing and a written document.
4
A written emergency plan. Leader roles, predetermined options, means of communication, exit points and evacuation routes marked on a map, emergency contacts in a waterproof container, scheduled review date.
5
Trip plan, region and local hazards. Leave the trip plan with a designated contact who will alert authorities if you are overdue, and notify them on your return. Know the access points, exits, local services and their limits. Check the diseases and hazards of the region and plan vaccination early enough for it to be effective.
